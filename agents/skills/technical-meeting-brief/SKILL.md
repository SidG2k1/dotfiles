---
name: technical-meeting-brief
description: "Use when the user is preparing for a technical meeting or shares a meeting transcript or notes. Produces a prep brief, or a summary of decisions, rationale, unresolved questions, and action items."
---

For preparation, check the relevant design, recent work, and discussion. Identify the decisions likely to need attention and the specific context the user should review; avoid guessing an agenda from a participant's title alone.

For a recap, preserve technical decisions, their rationale, constraints, open questions, and action items with owners only when supported. Separate suggestions from commitments, and distinguish explicit agreement from the agent's interpretation. Explain how the meeting changes the design when relevant.

Cross-reference related discussion or code when requested or needed to resolve an ambiguity. Omit unrelated personal discussion. Produce one complete technical summary rather than several partial versions.

Draft follow-up questions for consequential ambiguity. Do not schedule meetings or modify shared documents unless authorized.
